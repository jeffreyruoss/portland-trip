Trail run picker

Two run days: Tue Sep 29 afternoon (car at PDX ~1:30pm, sunset 6:55, sleep in Centralia) and Wed Sep 30 (start and end in Centralia — you're there both nights). Pick a day, set how far you'll drive, and the cards tell you whether it fits and when you'd arrive. The third option, Wed from Leavenworth, is the Enchantments alternative and only works if you swap the Centralia nights for Leavenworth.
