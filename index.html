Trail runs

Tue Sep 29 · Columbia River Gorge (east of Portland)

Wahkeena – Multnomah Falls Loop Pick

~5 mi · ~1,600 ft · 35 min from PDX

The classic. Waterfalls the whole way. Start at the Wahkeena lot so you hit the Multnomah crowds at the end, not the start. Add the Devil's Rest spur for ~8 mi / 2,400 ft.

Multnomah Falls I-84 lot has used timed-entry permits in summer. Usually done by late Sep — confirm on recreation.gov.

Angel's Rest

~4.8 mi out-and-back · ~1,500 ft · 30 min from PDX

Closest Gorge trailhead to town. Steady climb to a rocky viewpoint over the river. Quieter than Multnomah. Map

Dog Mountain (WA side)

~7 mi loop · ~2,800 ft · 1 h from PDX

Big vert, big views. Cross at Bridge of the Gods ($3 toll). Permits are only required on spring weekends. Map

Eagle Creek to Punchbowl / Tunnel Falls

4–12 mi out-and-back · gentle grade · 45 min from PDX

Runnable, cliff-edge trail with cables. Punchbowl Falls at ~2 mi, Tunnel Falls at ~6 mi. NW Forest Pass or $5 day fee at the lot. Map

Wed Sep 30 · Issaquah Alps / North Bend (east of Seattle on I-90)

Tiger Mountain – Poo Poo Point Pick

~7 mi round trip · ~1,800 ft · Issaquah

Steady forested climb to a paraglider launch meadow with views over Lake Sammamish. Chirico Trail is the direct route; High School trailhead is the mellower one. Map

Discover Pass ($10/day, sold at lot) at DNR trailheads.

Rattlesnake Ledge / Ridge

4 mi (Ledge) up to 11 mi (Ridge traverse) · North Bend

Best view-per-mile in the area. Ledge is 2 mi up with 1,160 ft; continue along the ridge for a real run. No pass needed at Rattlesnake Lake. Map

Cougar Mountain

36 mi of trail · rolling · Issaquah / Bellevue

Mellow, mossy, endless loop options. Best for a relaxed run after Gorge legs. Red Town or Sky Country trailheads. Free (King County). Map

Mount Si

8 mi round trip · 3,150 ft · North Bend

The big one. Relentless climb, great summit. Only if you want to hurt. Discover Pass. Map

Overnight stops

Tue night · between Portland and Seattle

Centralia, WA Pick

~1.5 h from Portland · ~1.5 h to Seattle · cheapest

True halfway point. McMenamins Olympic Club is the fun pick: old hotel + bar + theater, rooms often under $100 (many with shared bath). Chain motels off I-5 are cheaper still.

Vegan: no all-vegan spot, but Hash (Centralia) has marked vegan items and Burgerville has a vegan burger.

Olympia, WA

~2 h from Portland · ~1 h to Seattle

Real town with a downtown, and it has an all-vegan restaurant (Wayside Café). Slightly pricier than Centralia, still well under Seattle.

Castle Rock / Kelso

~1 h from Portland

Cheap, but too close to Portland to earn the stop. Only worth it if you run late in the Gorge.

Wed night · Seattle side

SeaTac airport hotels Pick if flying SEA

Cheapest sleep in the metro, 5 min to terminal, free shuttles, easy rental return. Boring but efficient.

Issaquah / North Bend

Sleep near the trailhead if you'd rather run at dawn Thu and then drive. Suburban chains, moderate price.

Seattle proper

Capitol Hill or U District puts you next to the vegan restaurants. Expect $200+. Parking is a hassle with the rental.

Fly home from Seattle or Portland?

Answer: fly from SEA unless the one-way drop fee is more than ~$100. The drop fee is the whole decision — everything else is roughly a wash.

Rental2 days + one-way drop fee ($0 to $150+, varies by company/week)3 days, no drop fee
Extra cost—+1 rental day (~$50–80) + gas (~$25)

FlightAlaska, Southwest, American, Delta. ~$100–200 one-wayOften slightly cheaper (Frontier, Southwest). ~$110–170 one-way

How to price it

  • On Kayak, search pickup Portland Sep 29 → drop SEA Oct 1, then the same with drop Portland Oct 1. Compare across brands; drop fees vary wildly.
  • Pick up at a neighborhood office (not PDX) to skip the ~10% airport concession fee. Enterprise/Hertz downtown or on the eastside are MAX-accessible.
  • Check SEA → PHX Oct 1 and PDX → PHX Oct 1 on Google Flights.

All-vegan restaurants

Everything tagged 100% vegan is fully plant-based. Portland has lost a lot of vegan spots in 2024–26 (Blossoming Lotus, Sudra, Sweet Hereafter, Paradox, Vertical Diner, Virtuous Pie all closed) — confirm hours on Google or HappyCow the day of.
